CAC BUOC UP LEN HOSTING:

3. Vao thu muc 'public_html' (thu muc goc cua ten mien).

6. Sau khi upload xong, nhap chuot phai vao file zip tren cPanel va chon 'Extract' (Giai nen).
7. Kiem tra: File 'index.html', cac thu muc 'js', 'css', 'assets', 'Anh Bai Viet Khung 16-9'
   phai nam ngay trong thu muc public_html.
